Orbit Shift

Privacy policy & support

Effective October 31, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Who operates the game

Orbit Shift is operated by Human Relief International Inc. (HRI). This policy covers the Android game with package identifier org.humanreliefinternational.orbitshift.

An offline game

The game does not collect or transmit personal data to HRI. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, in-app purchases, social features or online leaderboards. The game does not request Internet access.

Progress and preferences

Your completed boards, current puzzle and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. You can reset saved progress in Options. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Android cloud backup is disabled for the game.

Optional personal backgrounds

You can choose a picture with Android’s system file picker or take a picture using your device’s camera application. The game receives only the picture you select or capture for this feature. It does not request broad access to your photo library or a direct camera permission.

A resized copy, up to 2048 pixels on its longest side, is stored privately on your device as a background. The app does not upload or share it. Temporary image files are removed after processing or cancellation. Use “Remove personal photo” in the background options to delete the stored copy. Removing that copy does not delete the original picture you selected.

The file-picker provider or camera application you use operates under its own privacy policy and settings.

Purchases and Google Play

Google Play processes the purchase and may process store, licensing and device information under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. The game does not receive your payment card information. See Google’s privacy policy.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, HRI receives your email address and anything you include in the message. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment details or private photos that are not necessary for your request. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address, subject to records that must be retained by law.

Children’s privacy

The game does not collect personal information from players of any age. Parents or guardians can contact support with questions.

Policy updates

Any changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date. If a future version changes the game’s data practices, this policy will describe those changes.
